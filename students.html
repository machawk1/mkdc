Advising approach / Shared standards

Questions first. Methods in service.

The strongest projects begin with a consequential information problem, then develop the technical and conceptual tools needed to study it rigorously.

01

Build from the question

Let the research question determine the archive, dataset, vocabulary, or system—not the other way around.

02

Make work inspectable

Record decisions, provenance, limitations, and transformations so findings remain understandable.

03

Leave useful infrastructure

Favor reusable methods, open artifacts, and durable scholarly systems that support the next inquiry.
