Section 03 / Teaching approach

Technical fluency with documentary judgment.

Students learn not only how information systems operate, but how to question their evidence, assumptions, exclusions, and maintenance.

01

Read

Trace the system

Follow data and documents through collection, transformation, retrieval, and presentation.

02

Build

Make an artifact

Use code, metadata, and models to turn an abstract information problem into inspectable work.

03

Reflect

Document the decision

Explain tradeoffs, uncertainty, provenance, and the responsibilities introduced by design choices.

“The aim is not simply to make a system work. It is to understand what the system makes possible to know.”
