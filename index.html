Section 02 / Research

Research organized around the web’s long memory.

Across projects, I examine what web archives contain, what they omit, and how their records can remain understandable as platforms, language, and audiences change.

01

Web archives

Improving the capture and replay of dynamic, personalized, and distributed web experiences.

  • Memento & time-based access
  • Personalized advertisements
  • Replay fidelity & context

02

Semantic change

Tracing how concepts and vocabularies evolve across domains, communities, documents, and time.

  • Provenance-aware methods
  • Metadata vocabularies
  • Knowledge organization

03

Scholarly infrastructure

Building open, interoperable systems for researchers, librarians, archivists, and students.

  • Digital libraries
  • Persistent identifiers
  • Open research tools
