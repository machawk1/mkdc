Section 03 / Common contexts

Route the conversation by intent.

A small amount of context helps make the first exchange more useful and makes it easier to identify a next step.

01

Research

Project or paper

Name the research question, relevant work, proposed contribution, and anticipated timescale.

02

Students

Advising fit

Share your interests, preparation, degree context, and connection to current projects.

03

Invitations

Talk or service

Include audience, format, dates, expected contribution, accessibility, and any deadline.

Section 04 / First message
